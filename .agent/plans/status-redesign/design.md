
- **Collapsible:** YUNIE alias/intros + How-to vào `details/summary` — mặc định đóng, bấm mới mở.

## 7. Animation & A11y

- Tabs switch: `opacity 150ms + transform 150ms`
- Card hover: `translateY(-1px) + shadow 150ms`
- Sticky nav: `background 150ms`
- Contrast: text #0f172a on #f8fafc = 16:1, primary #6366f1 on white = 4.6:1 (AA)
- Focus: `outline 2px solid #6366f1; outline-offset 2px`
- Keyboard: Tab, `/` search, Enter on tabs, ESC close modal
- Reduced motion: `@media (prefers-reduced-motion)` disable transforms
